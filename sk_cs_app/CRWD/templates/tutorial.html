Tutorial for Crowdsourcing Activity

Task Description

Thanks for taking part in our study! In this page, we will go over what you are asked to do as a participant.

Each task is made of two steps: a validation step and an annotation step.

Validation step

You are asked to verify that a series of triples <concept, predicate, concept> (3, on the right) based on an image (2, on the left) include useful information to answer the given question (1, at the top).
Useful triples can directly help answering a question or provide additional, contextual information that helps answering the question.

To do this, you will need to go through several steps which are shown for each triple. These steps will cover whether the triple is useful (Q1), whether the concepts are identified correctly (Q2), and whether the predicate is correct (Q3). Depending on your answers, some options might be deactivated or not visible.

Some questions might be more complicated than others. To help with that, we broke down the operations you will carry out. Keep reading and go through the information in this page.

Answering the Questions in the Validation Step

Q1 - Is the triple useful to answer the question?

This is a yes-no question that helps us understand whether a triple is necessary to answer the given question.

Q2 - Are the concepts in the triple properly identified in the picture?

If your answer to Q2 is 'no', you will be asked to provide your annotation for the concepts. You can do this in various ways.

Incorrect concept label

First, if the concepts are localised correctly (see red and blue rectangles drawn on the image) but the label (that is, the name of the concept) is incorrect, you can type in the text box the label you think is correct. While you type, you will also see some suggested options (in orange) as you type; some generic, some more specific. Please, pick the one you think is the most appropriate for the image and question.

Incorrect position of a concept

Second, if the concept has the correct label but the position is incorrect, you can click on 'Change Bounding Box' and directly draw over the image. To do so, you simply, click and drag over the object as shown in this short video. If you make mistakes at any time, click on 'Reset Bounding Box'.

Q3 - Is the predicate between the two concepts properly identified?

If your answer to Q3 is 'The predicate is incorrect', you will be asked to provide your annotation for the predicate. Similar to how you provide annotations for incorrect concept labels, suggestions for the predicates will be shown as you type. Please, pick the one you think is the most appropriate for the image and question.

Annotation Step

Unfortunately, the triples that you will see might be insufficient to answer the question given a certain image. For this reason, in this second step we ask you to contribute with new relevant triples (4).

The procedure is similar to the one of the validation step. For each triple,

  1. You need to specify the labels for the concepts (suggestions will be displayed as you type);
  2. You need to draw the bounding boxes by clicking on 'Set Bounding Box' and clicking and dragging over the image. The new bounding box will appear automatically. If you are unhappy with this, or want to revert the annotation, click on 'Reset Bounding Box'.
  3. You need to specify the predicate that places the two concepts into a relationship.

In some cases, you might want to add additional triples to better answer the given question. You can provide more annotations by clicking on the 'New Annotation' button. Then, you can follow again the steps above.

New triples can be discarded by clicking on 'Remove Annotation'.

Submitting

Once you are done with the current set of triples, click on the 'Submit Annotations' button to proceed to the step, the next image, or complete your submission and return to Prolific. You will be prompted to confirm your choice of submitting the validated and annotated triples.

Triple that have not been validated or annotated are marked as [TO DO]. Otherwise, triples are marked as [OK] automatically as you progress through the task.

Do not worry if this looks like a lot of information!

At any point in time, you can access a summary of these instruction while doing the task.
