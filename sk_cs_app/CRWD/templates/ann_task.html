Instructions for Annotation Step

Given the image and question below, you are asked to create a series of triples <concept, predicate, concept> which include useful information to answer to a given question.

Note that in some cases, you might see an answer for the given question. If that is the case, you should also take into account the answer when creating new relevant triples in this step of the task.

Beware, different questions (and answers) can occur for the same image.

The procedure is similar to one of the validation task. For each triple,

  1. You need to specify the labels for the concepts (suggestions will be displayed as you type);
  2. You need to draw the bounding boxes by clicking on 'Create Bounding Box' and clicking and dragging over the image. The new bounding box will appear automatically. If you are unhappy with this, or want to revert the annotation, click on 'Reset Bounding Box'.
  3. You need to specify the predicate that places the two concepts into a relationship.

In some cases, you might want to add additional triples to better answer the given question. You can provide more annotations by clicking on the 'New Annotation' button. Then, you can follow again the steps above.

New triples can be discarded by clicking on 'Remove Annotation'.

Triples that need to be created are marked as [TO DO].
Otherwise, new triples are marked as [OK] automatically.

Once you are done with the current image-question pair, click on the 'Submit Annotations' button to proceed.
