Instructions for Validation Step

Given the image and question below, you are asked to verify that a series of triples <concept, predicate, concept> include useful information to answer to a given question.

Note that in some cases, you might see an answer for the given question. If that is the case, you should also take into account the answer when validating the triples shown in this step of the task.

Beware, different questions (and answers) can occur for the same image.

The validation will include the following aspects:

  1. The usefulness of the triple to answer the question;
  2. The correctness of the concepts identified in the image;
    • If incorrect, you can change a concept's label or re-draw its bounding box by clicking on 'Change Bounding Box' and clicking and dragging over the image. The new bounding box will appear automatically. If you are unhappy with this, or want to revert the annotation, click on 'Reset Bounding Box'.
  3. The correctness of the predicate.
    • If incorrect, you can change a predicate's label by typing the new one.

Triples without validation are marked as [TO DO].
Otherwise, triples that have been validated, are marked as [OK] automatically.

Once you are done with the current image-question pair, click on the 'Submit Annotations' button to proceed.
